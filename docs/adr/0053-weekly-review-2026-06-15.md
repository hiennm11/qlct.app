# ADR-0053: Weekly Review Card on Home (Epic 4)

**Status:** Accepted 2026-06-15
**Type:** Contract-ref (UX analytics insight, không schema/dependency/release policy change)
**Linked contract:** `docs/specs/weekly-review-contract.html`

## Context

Monthly Review (ADR-0021) cadence 30 ngày — quá thưa cho engagement ngắn hạn.
Cần 1 lớp insight 7 ngày ngay trên Home để tạo lý do user mở app mỗi tuần, không chỉ khi cần nhập giao dịch.

## Decision

Thêm `WeeklyReviewCard` widget + `WeeklyReviewViewModel` + `WeeklyReviewBuilder` + `WeeklyReviewData` model.
Runtime-only (mirror Monthly Review ADR-0021) — không persist, không migration, không backup schema bump.

**Architecture:**
- 10th `ChangeNotifier` (sau `AppSettingsViewModel` ADR-0050), wired qua `ChangeNotifierProxyProvider<ExpenseViewModel, WeeklyReviewViewModel>` trong `main.dart`.
- Direct DataSource injection (Transaction + Budget + Recurring + Category) — không inject cross-VM (mirror ADR-0006 §Recurring circular guard).
- 3-state (loading/loaded/error), `empty` derive từ `data.lowDataState` (ADR-0052 §3.1 lesson).
- Placement: giữa `BudgetOverviewWidget` và `TransactionListWidget` (HomeScreen.dart).
- Tap-through mirror `StatsWidget` pattern (ADR-0008) — setDateRangeFilter + scroll.

**Week boundary**: Mon-Sun (ISO 8601), cố định.
**Compare semantics**: same-period previous compare cho current week in progress (mirror ADR-0021 §Compare Period); full-week cho past week.
**Investment semantics**: spending-only cho totals/daysLogged/budgetRisk/topCategory; all categories cho largestTransaction (grill Q4 deviation, weekly cadence khác monthly) + upcomingRecurringCount.

**Self-detection 3 câu hỏi (CLAUDE.md /write-adr rule)**:
1. Schema bump? Không — runtime model, không DB table, không backup.
2. Dependency / layer change? Không — dùng DateTime + intl đã có, VM/Builder/DataSource pattern đã có ở Monthly Review.
3. Release policy / versioning change? Không.

→ **Contract-ref** (5-15 dòng), link contract HTML làm đặc tả UI/logic.

## Consequences

- ~5 file mới: `models/weekly_review_data.dart`, `services/weekly_review_builder.dart`, `viewmodels/weekly_review_viewmodel.dart`, `widgets/weekly_review_card.dart` (hoặc trong `lib/views/`), `test/unit/weekly_review_builder_test.dart` + widget test.
- 1 `ChangeNotifierProvider` entry trong `main.dart` (ProxyProvider 10th).
- 0 schema migration, 0 dependency change, 0 release policy change.
- Glossary mới (Phase 5): "Tuần (weekly)", "Review tuần", "WeeklyReviewData", `lowDataState`.
- `lowDataState` 3-state enum (empty/low/normal) — reusable pattern cho future analytics cards.
